ANIMAL WARRIORS - 3D LOOK TEST
=====================================
How to open and play it on your PC (Godot 4.7.2)

1. Unzip this folder somewhere easy, for example:  C:\Goldenmayhem\AnimalWarriors
2. Open Godot (Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe).
3. In the Project Manager click  Import  (top left).
4. Browse to the unzipped folder, pick the file  project.godot , click  Open , then  Import & Edit.
   (First time only: Godot spends a few seconds importing the pictures and sounds.)
5. Press  F5  (or the Play triangle, top right) to start the game.
6. Pick ARENA CAMERA (wide) or CLOSE CAMERA (Street Fighter distance).
   Tap the "You play" button first if you want to be the Kangaroo instead of the Hyena.

CONTROLS
  Keyboard:  A / D = move      W = aim up      S = aim down (hold on a thin platform to drop through)
             SPACE = jump (3 jumps: ground + 2 in the air; also wall jump)
             J = light attack  K = heavy attack (HOLD K to charge)   L = dodge
             H = grab the falling axe / throw it
  Mouse:     you can click the on-screen buttons too (they are the phone controls).
  Controller: left stick, A jump, X light, Y heavy, RB dodge, LB grab/throw.

WHAT EACH ATTACK DOES (light = J, heavy = K)
  Light on the ground:  jab (no direction), uppercut (up), low sweep kick (down)
  Light in the air:     flying kick, flip kick (up), stomp (down)
  Heavy:                signature move (Hyena FRENZY combo / Kangaroo TAIL KICK)
                        up = Sky Launcher, down = Ground Slam, down in air = Meteor Pound
  With the axe:         Axe Chop (light), Axe Riser (up), Axe Cleave (heavy). Press H to throw it.

ARENA: one flat floor, nothing to fall off. Get launched past the screen edge = K.O.
RULES: 3 lives each. Damage % goes up when you are hit; the higher it is, the further you fly.
Fly off the screen edge = K.O.  A health potion drops now and then (takes 25% off).

Nothing in this folder contains passwords or keys.
