# Animal Warriors: Character Bible
Source: the 55 reference sheets in `animal-warriors/Reference_Sheets/`, each read section by section at full size.
Every sheet has the same 11 sections: name card, colour palette, 5-view turnaround, 8 facial expressions, 8 action poses, 6 hand close-ups, 3 outfits, equipment, species anatomy close-ups, armour/gear close-ups (+ sometimes design notes).
Shared kit on every warrior: bronze pauldron on the left shoulder, chest strap with buckle, studded belt, layered leather skirt, leather bracers. Magic is usually **blue**, but not always: the Lion and Tiger cast **gold** magic (checked per character below).
Shared 3 outfits: Standard Warrior (balanced) · Light Scout (hooded cloak/scarf, lighter leather) · Heavy Armour (full bronze plate, cape).
Shared items: shield (each has its own emblem), red health potion, blue mana potion, open + closed spellbook, crystal orb, magic amulet, travel bag.
The 8 action poses on every sheet: walking, running, combat stance, blocking, weapon attack, casting magic, reading spellbook, celebrating.

**Sheet issues** list mistakes or mix-ups found on a sheet that need a decision.
"Game notes" in each entry are my suggestions for the arena brawler, for the owner to approve. Everything else is read straight off the sheet.

## 006 Elephant Warrior
- **Card:** Elephant (Elephantine). Role: Warrior / Battle Mage. Height ~3.2 m (10'6"). Build: massive, muscular.
- **Traits (sheet):** elephantine strength and endurance; trunk (grip, feel, breathe); tusks (natural weapons); large ears (hearing, presence); armoured warrior, master of the war hammer, also knows magic.
- **Palette:** grey-brown skin + shadow, ivory tusks/nails, gold-bronze armour, iron/steel metal, brown leather, deep brown cloth, highlight accent, blue magic.
- **Weapon:** war hammer: square stone/metal head reinforced with iron bands and rivets, long wrapped haft, balanced bronze pommel. Held **two-handed diagonally across the body** in front and ¾ views.
- **Shield:** round bronze shield embossed with an **elephant head**.
- **Turnaround:** front / ¾ front / side (trunk hangs forward, tusks curve up) / ¾ back / back. The back view shows **two straps crossing in an X** across the back.
- **Expressions:** neutral, happy, excited (mouth wide open), surprised (alarm marks), confused (?), worried (sweat marks), angry, confident (sparkle).
- **Action poses:**
  - Walking: hammer lowered in one hand.
  - Running: charging forward, kicking up dust.
  - Combat stance: low wide crouch, hammer two-handed.
  - Blocking: round shield up, yellow impact flash.
  - Weapon attack: overhead hammer swing with a motion arc.
  - Casting magic: open palm forward with a blue spiral rune ring, hammer in the other hand.
  - Reading spellbook: book glowing blue.
  - Celebrating: hammer raised high, fist pumping, cape on.
- **Hand close-ups:** open palm, fist, pointing, thumbs up, peace sign, weapon grip (on hammer haft).
- **Outfits:** Standard (bare chest, gear); Light Scout (brown hooded mantle and scarf); Heavy Armour (full riveted bronze plate and cape).
- **Equipment:** war hammer, elephant shield, health potion, mana potion, open spellbook (blue magic circle), closed spellbook (gold star), crystal orb on bronze stand, amulet (blue gem), travel bag.
- **Anatomy close-ups:** head and face, amber eye ("intelligent, focused"), large ear ("keen hearing"), trunk ("strong, flexible, precise grip"), padded foot with nails, muscular torso.
- **Gear close-ups:** rounded riveted shoulder armour, riveted leather bracer, heavy belt with round buckle, layered skirt/tassels, hammer head, hammer pommel.
- **Game notes:** slow, very tanky bruiser. Basic attack: short-range hammer smash with a ground crack. Block: elephant shield. Super: hammer slam shockwave or trunk trumpet blast that knocks back. Biggest body in the roster, so he needs a large hit area.

## 001 Lion Warrior
- **Card:** no stat card on this sheet (no height/role listed). Banner layout: "Lion Warrior: Character Reference".

  - Running: on all-fours-style lean, no weapon.

  - Reading spellbook: book glows gold.
  - Celebrating: fist raised, roaring, with ! marks.
