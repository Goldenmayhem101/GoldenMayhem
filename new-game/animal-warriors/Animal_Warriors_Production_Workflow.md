## Resume workflow

1. Read this list and the production checklist CSV. Start at the first Pending entry, keeping five characters per batch.
2. Reuse the original warrior portrait when one exists and the established reference sheet style. Maintain the same face, colors, markings, armor, weapon, and species details across panels.
3. Create one separate landscape reference sheet per character. Use warm ivory background, bold black ink, fine hatching, cel shading, bronze shoulder armor and brown leather gear. No live-animal photos, inset reference photos, website UI, or watermarks.
4. Include full-body front, three-quarter, side, back and standing views; eight expressions (neutral, happy, excited, surprised, confused, worried, angry, confident); action poses (walking, running, combat stance, blocking, weapon attack, casting magic, reading spellbook, celebrating); gestures (open palm, fist, pointing, thumbs up, peace sign, weapon grip).
5. Include standard warrior, light scout and heavy armor outfits; main weapon, shield, health and mana potions, open and closed spellbooks, crystal orb, amulet and travel bag; color palette and species/armor closeups. Adapt poses and gestures to the creature while retaining its identity.
6. Inspect each sheet for missing panels, anatomy, identity and readable labels. Record any needed corrections. Created status means an image was generated, not that every panel is a precise animation model.
7. Save each completed sheet with its number and animal name. Update CSV status and pause checkpoint after every batch. Retain earlier files and refresh the ZIP when requested.
