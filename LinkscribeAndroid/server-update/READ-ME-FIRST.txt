ONE CHANGE TO YOUR WEBSITE (needed before the app can work)

Your Linkscribe website needs one small update so the phone app is allowed to talk to it.
This server.js has three changes: it allows requests from the Android app, it limits free transcripts to 2 every 2 hours per phone, and it lets Google confirm watched videos (so people can earn 1 more by watching a video). The website itself looks the same.

1. Open your LinkScribe repository on GitHub (Goldenmayhem101/LinkScribe).
2. Click the file server.js, then the pencil icon (Edit).
3. Select everything (Ctrl+A), delete it, and paste the full contents of the server.js file from this folder.
4. Click "Commit changes". Render redeploys by itself in about 2 minutes.
5. Check it: open https://YOUR-ADDRESS.onrender.com/api/config in your browser. You should see something like {"hasSharedKey":true,...}

Do NOT paste your Gemini key anywhere except Render's Environment settings.

After it redeploys, open https://linkscribe-1.onrender.com/api/quota in your browser. You should see something like {"limit":2,"windowHours":2,"left":2,...}
