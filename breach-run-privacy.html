Breach Run Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026 · Developer: Goldenmayhem

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Breach Run ("Breach Run", "the game") on Android, published on Google Play by Goldenmayhem ("we", "us").

The short version: Breach Run has no accounts and we don't collect your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information, including your advertising ID, to show and measure those ads.

Information stored on your device

Your chapters, coins, stars, upgrades, skins, boosts, Daily Challenge streak and settings (music, sound, vibration, reminder) are saved only in the app's storage on your phone. We do not receive or keep a copy. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this progress.

Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

Breach Run is free and supported by ads, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. When ads load or play, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

DataExamplesWhy it's used
Device or other IDsAndroid advertising ID, app set IDShowing ads, limiting repeat ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud

This information is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. We do not sell personal information, and we only receive aggregated ad reports (for example, how many ads were shown) that do not identify you.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Personalized ads: On most Android phones, open Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads) to delete or reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. Ads that revive you or double a reward only play when you tap a "Watch an ad" button. A short ad may also appear between some runs.
  • Delete your game data at any time by uninstalling Breach Run or clearing its storage in your phone's app settings.
  • If you are in a region with privacy laws such as the EU, UK or certain US states, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data. Because we don't hold personal data ourselves, requests about ad data are best made through Google, but you can always contact us and we will help.

Internet connection

The game can be played offline. When you are online it may load its text fonts from Google Fonts, and as with any website request, Google receives your IP address and basic device information to deliver them.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, your phone shows one notification a day when the new Daily Challenge is ready. It is scheduled on your phone; we do not send or receive any data for it. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in your phone's app settings.

Children

Breach Run is a general-audience action game with cartoon zombie violence and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Security and data retention

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Breach Run handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Big changes will also be noted in the game's Google Play release notes.
